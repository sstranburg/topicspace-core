How to read this
Narrative How strong and active the story is around a company right now.
5D Relative Return How the stock performed vs its benchmark over the last 5 trading days. Positive = outperforming.
NDS — Narrative Dislocation Score Story strength minus price confirmation. High = strong narrative, market not paying up. Near 0 = aligned. Negative = price is ahead of the story.
